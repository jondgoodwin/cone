Cone
The Cone library

3D

Shapes, meshes, animation, physics, rendering, XR, worlds and interfaces for three-dimensional programs.

Shapes and sculpting

  • sdf: Signed distance fields: shapes as functions from a point to its distance from the surface.
  • sdfmesh: Any signed distance field sampled on a grid and made into a manifold mesh of quads, on the CPU or the GPU.
  • sculpt: Edit-mode tools on polygon meshes: bevel, subdivision, multires and the like, rebuilt from Blender's manual.
  • sculptbrush: Sculpt Mode's brushes and stroke engine on a fixed-topology mesh; a recipe of strokes replays to the same bits.
  • shapefit: Deterministic instruments for deciding how to shape a mesh: fits, landmark alignment and measures.
  • skinmesh: A quad mesh grown from a graph of nodes and edges, with a rig from the same graph.
  • quadremesh: Automatic quad retopology: a mesh of triangles or polygons becomes a mesh of quads at a face count asked for.
  • morphogen: Form that arises from growth rules (morphogenesis), made as graphs of points.
  • starship: Elizabeth's "Starship 1", the skeletal dragon ship, reproduced.

Meshes and geometry

  • geomath: The numbers 2-D and 3-D worlds are built from: vectors, matrices, quaternions, colours.
  • mesh: Surfaces made of polygons in the two forms the industry keeps them in: indexed triangles and a half-edge polygon mesh.
  • spatialindex: Where things are, asked three ways: which boxes a ray, a box, a ball or a frustum meets.
  • autolod: Levels of detail for any mesh, made automatically, ending in an octahedral impostor.
  • gltfimport: glTF 2.0 models read into plain data: meshes, materials, a node tree, skins and animations.

Procedural nature and surfaces

  • noise: Coherent noise for displacement and procedural textures, the same on the CPU and (mostly) bit for bit on the GPU.
  • noiserock: Rocks as meshes, from a sphere cut by planes and roughened by noise.
  • weberpenn: Trees grown by Weber and Penn's parametric method, as a skeleton of stems and then meshes.
  • leafrosette: Low plants that grow as a ring of blades from one point, as meshes.
  • groundcover: Lush, lived-in ground from rules.
  • terraingen: Landscapes from parameters: a terrain described as data, then eroded and made.
  • terrainlod: Ground that refines itself with distance, over any height function or heightmap tiles.
  • oceanwaves: An FFT ocean, calm to storm, that can be stepped, queried and drawn.
  • analyticdaylight: The light of a clear day and its night as functions of the time of day: sun, moon, sky colour.
  • boidflock: Flocks of small birds flying round a home point over rising and falling ground, and how each flaps.
  • surfacepatterns: Procedural patterns that fill a tile with a recognisable surface (boards, shingles, stone, bark, brick) and its material maps.
  • pbrmaterial: What goes into a physically based material: its parameters, and textures of them baked from procedural noise.

Animation and rigs

  • bonepose: A skeleton as data, a pose of it, forward kinematics and two-bone inverse kinematics.
  • poseik: Inverse kinematics, foot placement, look-at, hand reach, pose blending and a small animation state machine.
  • animlayers: Layered animation on top of a base pose.
  • keyframetracks: Property animation: keyframes with easing, grouped into clips, played with cross-fades, layers and events.
  • tweenstack: Animators that run over time and write the value of a field each tick.
  • creaturerig: Any creature's skeleton from data, and the weights that bind a mesh to it.
  • facerig: A face that is alive, for any face mesh described by data.
  • handpose: Five fingers that can hold, turn, press and knead: grip shapes and hands on handles.
  • humanbody: People of game quality as data: measures, a look and an outfit, made into a skinned body.
  • livingbody: Bodies that look alive: breathing and the other small continual motions of a standing person.
  • walkgait: A phase-driven biped walk with feet planted on the ground, an idle layer and a hard-landing crouch.
  • leggedgait: Gaits as data: a footfall pattern for N legs, and what goes with it.
  • wingflight: Wings and flight for any rig with wing chains.
  • strandflow: Strands in a flow: hair, manes, tassels and tentacles moving in air or water, drawn as cards or tubes.
  • splinetrack: Anything that runs on a track: splines, junctions and switches, and what moves along them.

Physics

  • rigidcollide: The collision half of a Jolt-like rigid-body engine: shapes, broad phase and contacts.
  • rigiddynamics: The solver half of a Jolt-like rigid-body engine: a World of bodies, joints and queries stepped at a fixed rate.
  • ragdollrig: Ragdolls for bonepose's skeletons over rigiddynamics, limp or powered towards a pose.
  • xpbdsoftbody: Cloth, ropes, chains and inflated envelopes by extended position-based dynamics.
  • clothrope: Cloth and rope that push and pull on the world, joining xpbdsoftbody and rigiddynamics.
  • garmentdrape: Garments draped on a body, with cloth materials: weave, sheen, wear and dirt.
  • buoyancy: Bodies that float, and a boat from data: hull, engine, rudder.
  • wheelvehicle: Anything on wheels, from data: engine, gearbox, differential, suspension.

Rendering and the GPU

  • vulkan: Raw bindings to Vulkan 1.3, written by hand from the specification.
  • gpu: Cone's own thin GPU layer, shaped like WebGPU's objects, with Vulkan underneath.
  • gpuwork: Handing wide, repetitive work to the GPU: kernels run per invocation, data moved only when it must.
  • render: Drawing lit 3-D meshes on the GPU: cameras, lights, materials and image-quality passes.
  • vfx: Visual effects drawn by the GPU, beginning with stateless particle emitters.
  • effectsfx: Effects at the standard of the best shooters, defined as data and drawn by the GPU.
  • msdftext: Text for the world, sharp at every size and angle, from TrueType fonts as an MSDF atlas.
  • vector2d: 2-D vector graphics drawn on the GPU, anti-aliased, with text.
  • diagramlayout: Diagrams from data, laid out and routed, drawn in 2-D by vector2d and in 3-D by render.

XR

  • openxr: Raw bindings to OpenXR 1.0 and 1.1, the part a headset's Vulkan swapchain needs.

Worlds, input and interfaces

  • window: A window to render into, opened through SDL, and the glue of a render loop around it.
  • sdl: Raw bindings to SDL3: video, windows, Vulkan surfaces, clocks, the event queue and audio streams.
  • input: The keyboard and the mouse as a program reads them: raw events, state, and named actions with bindings.
  • controls: Cameras steered by input actions, giving back a pose.
  • frame: The loop a world runs in: the owner calls the world, and the world never owns a 'while running'.
  • worldinteract: Action scenarios for a world as pure logic: doors, targeting, held things, properties, events and triggers.
  • navmesh: Finding the way: a navigation mesh, A* over it, funnel paths, raycasts, off-mesh links and steering.
  • npcbehave: What a non-player character decides and how it moves: behaviour trees, utility, state machines and steering.
  • editgizmo: Editing 3-D in place: pick by ray, move/rotate/scale handles, snapping, selection outlines, undo and redo.
  • uilayout: The layout and interaction core under widgets, for screen overlays and panels placed in a 3-D world.
  • uipickers: The controls an editor needs and uilayout lacks, and a property inspector built from a description of fields.
