Audio and voice

Mixing and playing sound, and speaking with a local model.

Sound

  • audiomixer: A real-time mixer, positional audio and procedural synthesis, written in Cone with no C.
  • audiodevice: audiomixer's output to the speakers, over SDL3's audio streams.

Speech

  • voiceaudio: The microphone and speaker of a spoken conversation, with voice activity detection.
  • speechtotext: NVIDIA Parakeet speech recognition on the GPU: 16 kHz samples in, text out.
  • texttospeech: Kokoro 82M text to speech on the GPU: English text in, 24 kHz samples out.
  • speechkernels: The GPU kernels for Kokoro, run through gpuwork.
  • englishphonemes: English text to phonemes for Kokoro, with its own spelling rules where misaki would call espeak.
  • voicechat: A spoken conversation with a local Qwen3: microphone, Parakeet, Qwen3, Kokoro, speaker.
