The Guide

The Guide gets you writing real Cone programs, quickly. By its end, you will be writing programs that divide their work and put every core of your machine to it.

It covers the fundamentals, in four stretches, and then shows how a program grows:

  • The basics: a first program, then variables, types and expressions.
  • Control flow: blocks and conditionals, loops, and functions.
  • Data types: numbers and strings, tuples, the structs and enums you build your data from, the collections (lists and dictionaries) that hold it, and the method patterns that let a struct of yours behave like a built-in type.
  • Concurrency: actors, which work side by side and are called like methods, and parallel each, one loop on every core.
  • Growing a program: more files, submodules, a library of your own, and tests, all built with Congo.

You have probably met most of these ideas before, in some form, so the pages move quickly. Where Cone does something its own way, a page slows down to show you. Every complete program can be run, and changed, in the playground, with nothing to install.

After the Guide come the parts where Cone is most itself: memory that is both safe and yours to manage, concurrency in depth, modularity, and safety and the boundary with C. They ask more thought of you, and they repay it. The Guide is the foundation they build on, so let's begin.
